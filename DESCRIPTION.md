Keep your Swell customers in sync with Brevo contacts, and send cart and order events that trigger your Brevo email and SMS automations. Customers who accept email marketing join your Brevo list, and leave it when they opt out.

Connect Brevo with an API key, enter the ID of your email list, and choose the events to send. Orders can also go to Brevo eCommerce for revenue reporting and segmentation.

- **Contacts that stay current.** New customer accounts become Brevo contacts with their name, and changes to their email, name, phone or opt-ins are sent as they happen.
- **Your list, kept up to date.** Customers who accept email marketing are added to your list, and removed from it when they turn email marketing off.
- **Phone numbers ready for SMS.** Turn on SMS sync, and customers who accept SMS marketing get their phone number in the contact's SMS attribute, in international format.
- **Events for your automations.** checkout_started, cart_abandoned, order_submitted, order_fulfilled and order_canceled, with the totals, currency, coupon and items, and for carts a link back to checkout. Turn each one on or off.
- **Orders in Brevo eCommerce.** Turn on order sync to send each order's number, total, status and products when it's placed, fulfilled or canceled.
- **Bring in every customer.** Import your existing customer accounts from the Actions menu. The import runs in the background, and each run shows on the app's Workflows tab.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Brevo, create an API key under Settings > SMTP & API > API Keys and note the ID of your email list. Paste both in the app settings, save, and choose Actions > Test connection. Then run Import existing customers to add the customers you already have.
